# Intro
Iron acetate is a great mordant for fabrics and can be used to color wood.
# How to make
Collect some rusty iron and put it in a non-metallic jar. Add vinegar, preferably enough to cover the iron. Put on a cloth over the opening of the jar and put a rubber band around it. Let sit for a few weeks.
## Important notes
Do not seal the jar. The process creates pressure that needs to be able to come out.
# As a mordant
Saddener. Binds well.
